Christmas Wheel: terms and conditions

Three Wise Monkeys January Bounce Back, December 2026.

1. Who runs it

The promoter is Pasini Leisure, 45 North Hill, Colchester, trading as Three Wise Monkeys Colchester, Three Wise Monkeys Ipswich and Other Monkey Brewing ("we", "us").

2. Who can play

Open to anyone aged 18 or over. Staff of Pasini Leisure can't play.

3. How to play

Scan the QR code on a Christmas Wheel sticker or on our TV screens, give your name, mobile number and email address, join Monkey Points (our loyalty scheme) and agree to receive marketing emails from us, then spin the wheel. You can unsubscribe from marketing at any time using the link in any email. This does not cancel a voucher you've already won, and your Monkey Points stay.

The wheel is open until 23:59 on Thursday 31 December 2026.

One spin per guest for the whole promotion. We check this against both your mobile number and your email address. Spins using more than one name, number or email address will be cancelled, along with any vouchers they produced.

4. The prize

Every spin wins the same prize: a voucher for £10 off the food on one bill. We email the voucher, with a unique code, to the address you give us straight after your spin.

5. Using your voucher

  • Valid from Friday 1 January 2027 until close on Sunday 31 January 2027. It can't be used before or after these dates.
  • Valid at Three Wise Monkeys Colchester, Three Wise Monkeys Ipswich and the Other Monkey Brewing taproom.
  • Your bill must be £15 or more before the voucher is taken off. The £10 comes off food on that bill.
  • Show the voucher email or code at the bar before you pay. Staff will check the code. Each code works once, and is then marked as used.
  • One voucher per bill. It can't be used with any other voucher, discount or offer, or split across more than one bill.
  • No cash alternative. No change, credit or refund is given for any unused part of the £10.
  • The voucher can't be swapped, transferred or sold, and it won't be replaced if the email is deleted. If you lose it, ask at the bar or reply to the email and we'll resend it to the email address you signed up with.
  • Normal opening hours, table availability and menus apply. Booking ahead is a good idea at busy times.

6. Your details

We store your name, mobile number and email in Square (our till and loyalty provider) and in our own records. We use them to run this promotion and Monkey Points, and to send you marketing emails you've agreed to. Ask at the bar or reply to any of our emails to have them deleted. Deleting your details also cancels any unused voucher.

7. General

We can cancel vouchers that we reasonably think were obtained unfairly, for example through multiple sign-ups or false details. We may change or end the promotion if something outside our control makes that necessary, and vouchers already issued will still be honoured. Our decision on any dispute about this promotion is final. These terms don't affect your legal rights.
